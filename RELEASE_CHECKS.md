# Checks before the next release

Things to try on a device before the next App Store or TestFlight release. Go through every item before you ship. Once the release is out, delete the items and keep the empty file.

- Install the App Store version, sign in, turn on flight mode, update to N+1 and open it. The sign-in screen shows. Turn flight mode off and wait up to 15 seconds. The app signs you in to the same account by itself.
- Repeat that, but sign in by hand to a different account before the 15 seconds pass. You stay in the account you chose, and the old account doesn't take over.
- While the app on the phone syncs a shift you just added offline, start or end an impersonation as admin. The shift stays with the account that made it, on the phone and on the server.
