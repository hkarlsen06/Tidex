# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tidex is for shift workers aged roughly 13-30 who need a practical way to understand what their work is worth before payday. They may be new to working life, juggling school or multiple jobs, or simply dealing with variable schedules, supplements, overtime, breaks, and employer-specific payroll rules.

Users come to Tidex to enter shifts quickly, keep a reliable work record, see expected earnings, and catch payroll mistakes without needing to understand payroll systems in depth.

## Product Purpose

Tidex exists to make shift pay understandable, predictable, and verifiable. The product turns work schedules, wage settings, supplements, breaks, tax settings, and reports into clear numbers users can trust.

Success means users can answer "what will I actually get paid?" with confidence, see how the calculation was made, and maintain a clean personal record of their shifts and earnings across devices.

## Brand Personality

Tidex should feel serious, transparent, and trustworthy. It should communicate control without intimidation, and confidence without pretending payroll is simpler than it is.

The product voice should be direct, plain-spoken, and calm. It should avoid cute framing, vague reassurance, and promotional overstatement. When calculations or data handling matter, the interface should show the reasoning instead of hiding it behind a polished total.

## Anti-references

Tidex should not look like a joke, a toy calculator, a gamified budgeting app, or a casual student side project. It should not rely on playful mascots, novelty illustration, meme-adjacent copy, exaggerated celebration, or decorative complexity that weakens trust.

It should also avoid opaque "magic number" presentation. Totals, reports, sync status, subscription limits, privacy choices, and payroll assumptions should not feel hidden, minimized, or manipulated.

## Design Principles

Make the math inspectable. Important totals need visible supporting details, clear assumptions, and a path to understand why a number changed.

Respect young workers without infantilizing them. The interface can be approachable, but it should treat users as capable people managing real income.

Prefer assertive clarity over decoration. Layout, copy, and motion should make decisions and data easier to scan, not merely make screens feel busy or branded.

Keep trust visible in the product surface. Privacy, sync state, payroll rules, exports, and errors should be explicit enough that users know what Tidex is doing with their work data.

Optimize for repeated use under normal life conditions. Fast entry, low cognitive load, strong defaults, and graceful correction matter more than impressive one-time onboarding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default to strong iOS accessibility support and WCAG AA-level contrast for public web surfaces. Preserve Dynamic Type behavior, VoiceOver labels, reduce-motion compatibility, and touch targets suitable for frequent mobile use.

Because pay data uses color and status, do not communicate meaning through color alone. Charts, badges, warnings, and payroll differences should remain understandable for color-blind users and in low-brightness phone use.
