Version 3.1.1 improves layouts for iPhone Duo and large text, and adds Siri actions for shift questions, shift changes and clocking out. Shifts and jobs can be opened from Spotlight. On iOS 27, Siri can also send or draft a message to a Tidex friend. iOS 26 remains supported.

Siri asks for confirmation before adding, moving or extending a shift. Earnings, shift changes and friends' schedules require the device to be unlocked. Friends' earnings are available only when that friend has shared them.

Sign in using the demo account credentials supplied in App Review Information. This account does not require a new-device email code. The demo jobs Harbor Café and City Library use USD intentionally, even on a Norwegian device.

You can add and edit shifts, inspect estimated earnings and pay settings, and use the shared calendar and messaging screens with the demo account.

Tidex is free and has no in-app purchases.
