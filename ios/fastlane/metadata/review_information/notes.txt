Version 3.1.0 migrates Tidex's backend from Supabase to Convex and moves authentication to Clerk. Existing account IDs, shifts, and settings are preserved. The submitted build is the build we intend to release.

The current App Store version, 3.0.1, continues to use Supabase. After approval, we will complete the final data migration and manually release 3.1.0. Push sending from Convex is disabled until that switchover to avoid duplicate notifications from both backends.

Sign in using the demo account credentials supplied in App Review Information. This account does not require a new-device email code. The demo jobs Harbor Café and City Library use USD intentionally, even on a Norwegian device.

You can add and edit shifts, inspect estimated earnings and pay settings, and use the shared calendar and messaging screens with the demo account.

Tidex is free and has no in-app purchases.
